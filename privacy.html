Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Tariff Buddy ("Tariff Buddy," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Tariff Buddy website, mobile app, and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Tariff Buddy, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address if you create an account or sign in.
  • Product and scan information, such as barcode values, product names, country-of-origin details, tariff estimates, saved products, and related lookup history.
  • Support information, such as messages you send us, feedback, or troubleshooting details.
  • Device and usage information, such as device type, browser/app version, pages or features used, timestamps, diagnostics, and general performance data.
  • Payment information, if paid features are offered. Payments are processed by third-party payment providers; we do not store full credit card numbers on our own servers.

Camera and Barcode Scanning

The Tariff Buddy app may request camera access so you can scan product barcodes. Camera access is used for scanning while you are using the feature. We do not use your camera for unrelated purposes.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide barcode scanning, tariff lookups, product comparisons, and saved-product features.
  • Create, maintain, and secure accounts.
  • Improve accuracy, reliability, design, and performance.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback.
  • Process purchases or subscriptions where applicable.
  • Detect abuse, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service.
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and security obligations.

Tariff and Product Data

Tariff Buddy provides product and tariff information for convenience and research purposes. Product, country, and tariff data may be approximate, incomplete, or change over time. Information you scan or save may be stored to provide app features and improve the service.

Cookies, Analytics, and Diagnostics

Our website or app may use cookies, analytics, crash reporting, and similar technologies to understand usage, measure performance, diagnose issues, and improve the service. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings where applicable.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information in the traditional sense. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate Tariff Buddy, including hosting, database, analytics, diagnostics, payment, email, customer support, and security providers. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Third-Party Services

Tariff Buddy may rely on third-party services for hosting, authentication, payments, analytics, product lookup data, or other app functionality. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, improve safety and reliability, and meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we may delete, anonymize, or securely retain it as required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no internet or electronic storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or object to certain uses of your personal information. You may also opt out of marketing communications if we send them. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Tariff Buddy is not intended for children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

International Users

If you access Tariff Buddy from outside Canada or the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of Tariff Buddy after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
